# Discovery Interviews

Semi-structured interviews run early in the project to understand how people preserve, revisit and share memories, before we committed to a concept. These fed directly into the Space-Cap direction (asynchronous memory drops, revealed together).

## Method

- **Format:** semi-structured, 1:1. Interviewers could adapt questions as long as each *area to measure* was covered.
- **Participants:** 10 total, recruited through personal networks.
- **Interviewers:** Shirish (5), Dong (2), Vim (3).
- **Round 1 (P01, P03, P04, P05):** open, general questions to see what habits exist before narrowing down.
- **Round 2 (P02, P06–P10):** the structured 14-area guide below, written from round 1 findings.
- **Analysis:** affinity mapping on the team Miro board → seven themes → Rumsfeld matrix → experience requirements (see `thematic-analysis.md`).

## Interview guide (14 areas)

| # | Area to measure | Core question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reflection habits | How often do you think back on past memories? Is there a typical time or situation? |
| 2 | Memory in everyday digital artefacts | Do you scroll back through old texts, chats or photos just to look? What prompts that? |
| 3 | Digital vs physical preservation | When something meaningful happens, do you photograph it, write it down, keep an object? Why that one? |
| 4 | Preservation actions taken | Walk me through what you actually do to preserve memories, with a recent example. |
| 5 | Techniques by medium | Does your approach change for a photo vs an object vs writing vs a keepsake? |
| 6 | Value and selection criteria | What makes something feel worth preserving versus letting go? |
| 7 | Positive vs negative memories | Does it matter if a memory is happy or difficult in whether or how you keep it? |
| 8 | Motivation to preserve | Why preserve memories at all? For yourself or for others? |
| 9 | Duration / permanence | Do you think about how long you want to keep something? |
| 10 | Effectiveness of current methods | How well do your methods work? Ever been disappointed revisiting something? |
| 11 | Physical memory: access & frequency | Where are physical mementos kept, and how often do you look at them? |
| 12 | Digital memory: access & frequency | How does that change for digital memories? Why? |
| 13 | Lessons over time | Has looking back ever changed how you feel about a memory? |
| 14 | Generational transfer | Has anything been passed down to you, or would you pass something on? |

## How this connects to user testing

Interviews answered *how people relate to memories*. User testing (see [`../../user-testing/`](../../user-testing/)) answered *whether our prototype works for groups*. The link between the two is set out at the end of `thematic-analysis.md`.
