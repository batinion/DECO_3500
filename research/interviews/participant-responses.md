**4. Preservation actions taken**

**10. Effectiveness of current methods**

P2: "I attempt not to go back to sad memories that I've captured with happy memories. I do go back to them."


**11. Physical memory — access & frequency**

P2: "So every year I have a new book and journal, which I do stuff on."


**12. Digital memory — access & frequency**

*Not addressed*


**13. Lessons and reflections over time**

P2: "No, because whenever I look back at past stuff, I've already realized what I've done wrong and what I need to change when I'm in the moment, when I'm writing it down. That's one of the reasons why I do it, because then I'm realizing when I look back at them, it's not really helping me in any way. P2: "Not really passed down to me directly, but we have a bunch of photo albums of me as a baby and like the whole family. when we were kids. But I have my own. I have 4 different photo albums of myself as a kid. Not here in India obviously, but yeah."

## P03

**5. Preservation techniques by medium**

Yes, differs by medium — photos catalogued, diary for narrative, binder for letters (despite degradation risk)


**6. Value and selection criteria**

Tied to relational/reflective significance — diary as daily "act of appreciation"; photo value increases through deliberate curation


**7. Positive vs. negative memories**

Painful periods documented far more sparsely at the time; distance brings peace rather than reprocessed pain


**8. Motivation to preserve**

Mixed — personal comfort primarily, but photo collection deliberately intended for nieces/nephews


**9. Duration/permanence**

Not directly asked; implied long-term/permanent, though paper documents flagged as degradation risk


**10. Effectiveness of current methods**

Not directly asked; implied effective — system has practical uses (e.g. tax records) and resurfaces forgotten details positively


**11. Physical memory — access & frequency**

Diaries on shelf, occasionally pulled down; boxed photos/letters accessed situationally (e.g. tax time)


**12. Digital memory — access & frequency**

Very frequent — Notes app is his central, regularly-updated digital record


**13. Lessons and reflections over time**

Yes — rereading the year his mother died brought comfort and surfaced forgotten details; pain becomes peace with distance


**14. Generational transfer**

Photo catalogue explicitly for nieces/nephews; keeps inherited recipes/handwriting; unsure if diaries/digital records will be useful to others
