# 0003: Collect memories across a semester, not in one sitting

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-19
- **Deciders:** Whole team

## Context

v1 collected every memory in a single session. Testers said that felt like a classroom
exercise rather than something they would use:

> "A lot of group memories build up over a season... As a one-day thing, I'd only use it in
> a classroom setting."

A second tester made the same point about a two-week road trip. Both pushed further than we
were willing to go, asking for continuous collection across a whole year.

## Decision

Collect prompted memories across a semester, tagged to the course week, rather than either
one sitting or an open year-round feed.

## Rationale

- Continuous uploading competes directly with Instagram and Snapchat, which is a fight we
  would lose and a product we would not be adding anything to.
- The interview theme of recreative curation says people only sort memories when something
  forces them to, which argues for prompts rather than an open feed.
- A semester has a natural endpoint, which the ritual needs.

## Consequences

- 33 course-tagged prompts were written, offered by week.
- Crews persist to disk, so a crew can live for weeks.
- This is the project's main claim to difference, and it is the claim with the weakest
  evidence behind it. In prototype test 2, "I would add something next week without a
  reminder" scored 3.1 of 5. Recorded as the open tension on the Requirements page.
